# Tasks: The medium the eye stands in reaches the image

**Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md) (SPEC-032) · **Architecture**: [architecture.md](architecture.md), binding · **Branch**: `feature/PRO-998-underwater-tint` · **Created**: 2026-08-31

42 scenarios, each in exactly one task. Measured, not relayed: `grep -cE "^  - FR-[0-9]+\.[0-9]+-S[0-9]+: " spec.md` prints 42 and `grep -oE "^  - FR-[0-9]+\.[0-9]+-S[0-9]+" spec.md | sort | uniq -d` prints nothing. Per phase: 19 · 17 · 6 = 42. **The phases are cut by what a test can be written against before its code exists**, so FR-2 splits three ways and FR-6 two: FR-2.6 is a reading over the shipped world needing nothing built and is therefore the *first* commit of the spec, and FR-6.2's page edits land with the loader because `documented_declaration_fields.rs` compares the three modding pages against `FIELDS_IN_THE_ORDER_THE_GUIDE_STATES` and a phase ending with the loader at fifteen names and the pages at thirteen ends **red**. Rigor is `high`: a phase's tests are authored and owned by a test author who has not seen the implementation, the implementation context never edits a test file, and a disputed failure goes to that author for exactly one of `test-correct` / `test-wrong` / `scenario-ambiguous`. **T01, T03, T09 and T13 are the test author's outright**, and T01 and T13 carry scenarios rather than a dash because what FR-2.6 and FR-6.1 require *is* a reading, with no production code for it to be written against.

**Two non-compiling windows, each one commit wide, and both open on an implementation commit** so the assertions that follow fail on an assertion rather than on a compile error (`testing.md` §2). **Window A opens at T02 and closes at T03**: `BlockDefinition` has no `Default`, so `tint` breaks every literal of it at once — `grep -rn "BlockDefinition {" --include=*.rs crates/ tools/ | grep -v "pub struct"` gives **24 sites across 21 files**, of which exactly two are not the test author's (`crates/mc-world/src/content/luau_declaration/mod.rs` and the bench at `crates/mc-world/benches/support/fixtures.rs`), leaving **22 test-owned constructions across 19 files**. **Window B opens at T08 and closes at T09**, and **the instrument is what to state here, not the number**. `grep "TerrainSnapshot {"` returns **15**; one is the declaration and **five are `-> TerrainSnapshot {` return-type signatures**, which an added field does not break — so a grep answers 14 test-owned sites and is wrong by five. The census that settles it is the compiler's: with `tint` on the struct and the two production publishers filled, `cargo check --workspace --all-targets --all-features --keep-going` names exactly **nine `error[E0063]`, one per test-owned literal, across nine files — three in `mc-render`'s own tests**, which the grep doubles because each of those three files holds a signature *and* a literal. `SimSnapshot` contributes no test-owned site at all: its only two literals are those publishers, and mc-sim's suite never stopped compiling. **Nine sites are nine edits, and no struct-update note is owed** — every literal spells all its fields (`grep "\.\."` finds none, and a `..` literal cannot raise `E0063`), so nothing here inherits `tint` silently the way phase 1's `sea.rs:242` does. **Let the compiler say when the census is complete, never a count**: an author working to a wrong N hunts sites that do not exist, and one working to a count at all stops when the tree compiles rather than when the census closes. **While the tree does not compile the gate cannot run**, so whoever authors tests inside either window runs both `cargo fmt --all -- --check` and `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings` directly at that severity: clippy is blind to formatting, and a green suite is no evidence about a lint.

**Five constraints every test author's brief must carry, each BINDING.** (a) **The tint is resolved from the live registry at every publish and never cached across ticks** — a cache passes every FR-2 reading, because each declares its own pose and renders one frame, and silently breaks FR-4.1-S1/S2/S4, whose whole content is that a *second* frame after a reload differs from the first. (b) **FR-2.3-S1, FR-2.4-S1 and FR-4.1-S3 are satisfied by a build that never writes the uniform at all** — measured, not predicted: forcing the reach to no tint reddens nine of the ten readings and FR-4.1-S3 is the one that survives, because it compares a frame before a refused reload against one after and with no tint anywhere those are equal. — a zero-filled buffer *is* `tint_reach = 0`, and their absolute halves are dry predictions that pass too. **The wiring witnesses are FR-2.1-S1…S6, FR-2.2-S1, FR-2.4-S2 and FR-4.1**, and all ten land in the same phase as the wiring; no shape of assertion inside the two identity scenarios recovers what a suite losing them would lose. (c) **`tint_reach` is carried as `1/D` and written as the literal `0.0` where there is no tint**, never as the reciprocal of a sentinel: `mix(a, b, 0.0)` returns `a` bit-exactly under all three forms a backend compiles it into, so there is no `if` and no second code path, which is what makes FR-3.1-S1 a byte-for-byte claim. The exclusive `> 0` floor on `tint_distance` is what keeps `1/D` finite, so that bound carries two independent reasons. (d) **The field list has three mirrors and each is compared whole and in order** — `luau_declaration_keys.rs:209`'s `fields_the_refusal_quotes` reads the list *out of* the observed refusal instead of filtering needles against a held copy, which is the repair for the failure `testing.md` §2 records (two mirrors held at six while the loader grew to nine, neither reddening). **Do not regress it**; FR-1.5-S1 asserts fifteen names in the loader's own order, so a missing name, an extra name and a reordering are three distinct failures. (e) **Every expected colour is derived by arithmetic in linear light and re-encoded**, from the declared colour, the declared distance, the surface's own colour and its **radial** distance from the eye — never copied from a run, never left as a frame-to-frame comparison alone.

## Notes

Out of scope and recorded, never built: caustics, refraction and surface distortion, a screen-space overlay, bubbles, a swim animation, a breath meter, drowning, sound, tinting the HUD or the debug overlay, any view of the surface from below, a depth-below-surface or biome or time-of-day gradient, a per-medium clear colour declared apart from the tint, the sky colour becoming content, sorting between translucent kinds, and `merges_with_self`. **PRO-999** holds the two shipped colour readers that each refuse the other's form as the only one there is; neither is touched here, and doing it inside a water spec would put a HUD change in a water spec. **PRO-952** must not be foreclosed: a tint pair and a later `render = "<method>"` must coexist on one declaration. **PRO-1000** holds the gate's own nextest invocation not carrying `--no-fail-fast`, which is why a red gate's count is slashed and why the bare count, not the flag, is what proves completeness. **The 0.38-block margin is debt either way** — a later change to `EYE_HEIGHT` or to the sea's depth kills submersion silently, and FR-2.6-S2 is the only thing that would say so. ADR-032 is already written into `docs/technical/decisions.md` by the architect phase and is not owed again at completion. **Never run `.prospect/scripts/sdd-next.sh` to read which phase is next**: it has no read-only mode and appends a `metrics.md` row on every invocation (PRO-995) — that row is added by hand, as this phase's was.
