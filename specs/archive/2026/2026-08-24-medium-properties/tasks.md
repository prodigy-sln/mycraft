44 scenarios, each in exactly one task. Measured, not relayed: `grep -cE "^  - FR-[0-9]+\.[0-9]+-S[0-9]+: " spec.md` prints 44 and `grep -oE "^  - FR-[0-9]+\.[0-9]+-S[0-9]+" spec.md | sort | uniq -d` prints nothing. Per phase: 11 · 16 · 6 · 11 = 44.

Rigor is `high`: a phase's tests are authored and owned by a test author who has not seen the implementation, and the implementation context never edits a test file. **T05 and T12 are the test author's outright, as are the two mirrors named in T02** — and **T05 lands before T06 or the phase deadlocks at first compile**, since widening `advance_player`'s door leaves the tree uncompilable until all five `Solidity` implementors also implement `Medium`, three of them in test files. While a tree does not compile the gate cannot run, so whoever authors tests in that window runs `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings` directly, at that severity (`testing.md` §2).

**Three constraints the test author's brief must carry**, because each bites only when someone writes a fixture (`architecture.md` Decisions 3 and 4): (a) every solidity fixture implements `Medium` as `VoxelMedium::NOTHING` **unconditionally, both halves** — never derived from the fixture's own solidity — where the falsifier is the existing collision suite staying green and the reporter is `player_collision.rs` (29 `jump` references) and `player_ground.rs` (37), while `player_motion.rs` has zero and reports nothing; (b) a ratio scenario asserts on `PlayerState::velocity` or on the displacement before it is added to a position, **never on `end − start`** — measured 425 mismatches of 512 at realistic replay coordinates against 0 of 512 on the displacement, the first already at a start of `0.3`, so the naive origin fixture is the one that passes; (c) the T05-before-T06 ordering above.
