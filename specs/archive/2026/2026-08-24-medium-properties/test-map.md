## Phase 1 mutation check

Eight mutations against the shipped loader, each reverted by hand and confirmed with `git diff --exit-code` before the next ran, every summary a bare count — so each green half is an observation rather than a cancellation. The first six ran over `luau_declaration_medium` and `luau_declaration_medium_refusals` from a baseline of `14 tests run: 14 passed`; the last two added `luau_declaration_medium_ceiling`, baseline 16. Two announced windows, and every red was named before the run — the first four by the test author, the last two by the implementer.

| Mutation | Reddened |
|---|---|
| `ScriptValue::Integer` arm deleted | 3 — the whole-number, the stated-zero and the negative-floor tests |
| finiteness check deleted | 3 — the NaN, the infinity and the `1e40` tests |
| `stated + 0.0` normalisation deleted | 1 — the signed-zero test |
| `swimmable` routed through `defaulting_to_solidity` | 1 — the solid fixture stating neither field |
| floor check deleted | 1 — the negative-floor test |
| `MOVE_RESISTANCE_BY_DEFAULT` moved `0.0` to `1.0` | 2 — both fixtures stating no resistance |
| ceiling clamped at `1e20` in the reader | 2 — the pair's `3.4e38` half, and the `1e30` fixture eight orders below it |
| finiteness check deleted, run over the pair | 1 — the pair's `3.5e38` half; its `3.4e38` neighbour is finite and unmoved |

**Nothing survived**, and two of the counts say more than that. The first reddens three tests rather than the one written for it, because `-1` and `0` reach the host as Luau integers too — a second witness that came free from writing fixtures in the language's own literals instead of all as fractions. The fourth reddens exactly one, which is what makes the *solid* fixture load-bearing: written non-solid it would have survived in silence, since `defaulting_to_solidity` and the constant agree wherever `solid = false`.

**The one missed prediction is the most useful entry here.** The implementer registered 1 for the `1e20` clamp and measured 2: `1e30` was authored for FR-2.1-S4's "unbounded above" and sits above that clamp, so it reddens too. Recorded as a miss rather than quietly corrected, because a prediction published only when it holds is not a prediction. What it showed is that a moved ceiling has **two witnesses at different resolutions** — `1e30` catches one that falls below a decade, the pair localises one to three percent — and neither subsumes the other. The last two mutations also separate the pair's halves: the clamp takes only the half that registers, the deleted check only the half that refuses, so the two are an instrument rather than one fact stated twice.

### What the Phase 2 fixtures are shaped to catch

- **Every FR-4 and FR-5 fixture is a declaration resolved through a real registry and a real `ResolvedVoxels`** (`support/medium.rs`), never a hand-written `medium_at`, which would be a second statement of the rule under test and would agree with itself forever. A declared medium is written over `x ∈ [8, 12)`, `z ∈ [0, 6)` with the feet at `(10.5, ·, 3.5)` — their transpose is outside — so a fold reading a box's z where it meant its x reports the unresisted answer.
- **Ratios are asserted on `PlayerState::velocity`, never on `end − start`**: 0 mismatches of 512 on the displacement against 425 of 512 on the subtraction, the first already at a start of `0.3`. Every declared resistance makes `1 + r` a power of two, so the epsilon is bounded above a measured error of nought and below the `0.75` blocks per second separating the nearest wrong divisor — except the one fixture written to break that property on purpose.
- **Three solidity fixtures answer `VoxelMedium::NOTHING` unconditionally, both halves** (`support/solidity.rs`, `support/chamber.rs`, `mc-client/tests/camera_lens.rs`). The falsifier is the pre-existing suite staying green through the adaptation, which holds only because a resistance of zero divides by one and is the velocity itself in every bit.

**Two predictions missed, published because one offered only when it holds is not a prediction.** M1 was called at 13 and gave 12: the `1e30` walk stayed green, because its control asks only that the walk be *slower* than air and a halved air is still vastly faster than `4.5e-30`. And M2 reddened `player_ground a_jump_asked_for_in_mid_air_does_nothing_to_the_fall`, which nobody named.

**The second miss is a correction to a premise `architecture.md` Decision 3 paid a page for, and it runs the opposite way.** That decision argues the `swimmable` half of the fixture rule is guarded by the suite — "those two files go red", naming `player_collision.rs` (29 jump references) and `player_ground.rs` (37) — while the `move_resistance` half "is inert" and is "the half that rots". Measured, by making the air itself buoyant and then resistant: **swimmable reddens one** pre-existing test, and `player_collision.rs` does not move at all, because nearly every jump in the suite is asked *from the ground* where `on_ground || buoyant` is already true; **resistance reddens 46**, across twelve files — every walk, every fall, every jump arc, the replay poses and a golden frame. The rule the document argues for is unchanged and still right, both halves unconditional. What has moved is which half it is protecting, by a factor of forty-six, and therefore which reporter is worth defending: a single `player_ground` test rather than two files. Reached by running, like every correction this spec has recorded.

## Phase 3 — The shipped sea
Phase 3 command: `cargo nextest run -p mc-sim --no-fail-fast`. One crate: every scenario is asserted against the world `REPLAY_SEED` generates and the registry `content/base` builds, both of which `mc-sim`'s own `tests/support` already reaches. **No number here is water's declared resistance** — it is read back out of that registry in `support/sea.rs`, and every threshold is arithmetic over that reading and over the physics constants; `TICK_DURATION`, `GRAVITY` and `JUMP_SPEED` are mirrored in the sea file as they are in eight other test binaries, and the mirror carries its own witness below, while `WALK_SPEED`'s is FR-6.1-S6's shore control.
